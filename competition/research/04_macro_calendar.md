### 2.2 How markets reacted historically
- Over 31 midterm cycles, S&P 500 averaged +2.9% in the 12 months before midterms versus 8.9% long-run, then +12.4% in the 12 months after. Since 1938 the S&P was up in the 12 months after midterms about 95% of the time. [V: Capital Group/Atranica summaries; the second-hand source is low quality]
- Midterm-year **October averages +3% (positive 73.7% of the time) and November +2.7% (78.9%)**, the best two months since 1950 [V: Fortune Sep 23]. One Substack claims every cycle since 1962 fell mid-Aug to Election Day (average -8.1%) with lows most often in October [V, low quality].
- Republican president with a Democratic Congress: S&P three-month returns about 1 point below average [V: Schwab summary]. Schwab, Morgan Stanley, BlackRock and J.P. Morgan all say equity impact is limited and rates/earnings dominate [V].
- From memory [M]: 2018 (split Congress) S&P +2.1% on Nov 7; 2022 election week was dominated by the Nov 10 CPI (S&P about +5.5% that day); 2014 and 2010 (Republican gains) were followed by rallies. **Nov 10, 2026 CPI is again in the post-election week.**

### 4.3 Who gets crushed
Long-duration and unprofitable growth, small caps (floating-rate debt), biotech (XBI), homebuilders, REITs, utilities, solar and storage, high-multiple software, and crypto and AI-speculative names. **Solar sits in both camps: blue-wave winner but rate-crushed.**

### 4.4 The hedging-through-longs angle
The same shock that hurts long-duration lottery tickets (oil up, then inflation, then Fed/BoJ/BoE hikes, then long yields up) **helps tankers/E&Ps/defence, Japanese banks and insurers, and US life insurers**. The mirror image (Hormuz reopens, yields fall) helps the growth tickets and airlines, and hurts tankers. Holding both sleeves is a natural barbell: whichever way the oil-yield chain breaks, one sleeve funds the other. [inference] Caveat: a **non-oil** yield spike (Treasury supply, yen, Japan fiscal) hurts growth and does not help tankers; only Japan banks/insurers, US life insurers and asset-sensitive financials hedge that case.
